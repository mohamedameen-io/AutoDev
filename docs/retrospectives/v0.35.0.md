# Release Retrospective: v0.35.0

**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Release tag:** v0.35.0
**Postmortem author:** AutoDev maintainers
**Triggering event:** Knowledge-base hygiene (Tier C of the v0.32.0 retrospective). v0.33 cleared plan-phase blockers and v0.34 cleared execute-phase blockers; the residual failure mode in the v0.32.0 fixture is now the knowledge store itself — a long-lived `applied_count=940, succeeded=0` entry continuously injected into every relevant role's prompt without ever preceding a successful task, plus critic-derived events with thin evidence bumping confirmations until promotion threshold was reached on noise.

## Summary
v0.35.0 closes Tier C by adding a soft-flag quarantine for chronically low-yield entries, a critic-evidence quality gate that screens abstention markers before confirmations bump, and a stricter promotion gate that requires both 10 confirmations and at least one observed success increment. The prerequisite that made C1 functional (`succeeded_after_count` is now actually written) ships alongside.

## 1. What shipped
- C1 prerequisite (`succeeded_after_count` writer): **landed** — `KnowledgeStore.inject_block_with_ids` returns the entry IDs that landed; the Orchestrator maintains `_injected_lessons_by_task` keyed by `(task_id, role)`; the `complete` transition in `src/orchestrator/execute_phase.py` drains the slot via `_credit_injected_lessons_for_task` which calls `KnowledgeStore.credit_task_success`.
- C1 (quarantine + audit JSONL): **landed** — `KnowledgeEntry.quarantined: bool = False` and `last_applied_at: datetime | None = None` added (default-safe deserialization preserved); `_evaluate_quarantine` and `_append_quarantine_audit` helpers added; `inject_block_with_ids` skips quarantined entries, sets `last_applied_at`, evaluates quarantine atomically under `plan_lock`, and writes one line to `.autodev/quarantine_audit.jsonl`. New ledger op `knowledge_entry_quarantined` emitted per flip.
- C2 (critic-evidence quality gate): **landed** — `_critic_evidence_quality(text)` classifier with module-level marker tuples (`_CRITIC_THIN_EVIDENCE_MARKERS`, `_CRITIC_NOISE_MARKERS`, `_CRITIC_MIN_EVIDENCE_CHARS=80`); `record_tournament_event` runs the gate before persisting and emits `critic_evidence_rejected` on rejection.
- C3 (promotion + 7-day decay): **landed** — `KnowledgeConfig.promotion_min_confirmations` default `3 → 10`; `_promote_if_qualified` adds the `succeeded_after_count > 0` conjunct and emits `knowledge_entry_promotion_rejected` with a typed reason; `_rank_with_ts` switches to a 7-day half-life curve anchored on `last_applied_at` whenever the entry carries that field and no per-event-type override applies.

## 2. What changed
Matches the v0.35.0 CHANGELOG entry:
- Quarantine: `quarantined=True` plus parallel audit JSONL line at `.autodev/quarantine_audit.jsonl`.
- Success crediting: `succeeded_after_count++` per task-complete transition via the Orchestrator's correlation map.
- Critic gate: thin or noise evidence is logged but not promoted to confirmations.
- Promotion: 10-confirmations floor AND at least one success required; ranking sees a 7-day decay on the confirmations weighting.
- New ledger ops: `knowledge_entry_quarantined`, `knowledge_lesson_credited`, `critic_evidence_rejected`, `knowledge_entry_promotion_rejected`.

## 4. What's NEW
- **Caused by the fix itself:** quarantine ratio threshold of `<0.1` over `applied_count > 10` is global; a genuinely-useful entry that landed in 11 prompts for 11 unrelated tasks none of which happened to need it will quarantine on the 11th injection. Soft-flag and the audit JSONL make this recoverable post-hoc (operator can flip the bit back), but the false-positive risk is real and only the next prod run measures it.
- **Below the most-urgent layer at the prior retrospective time:** Tier D (retries, budgets) and Tiers E–G (forensics, spec hygiene) are now at the head of the queue. v0.35 closes the knowledge-pollution loop; v0.36 attacks the retry-loop FSM, the architect budget escalator, and the operator-facing forensics surface.

## 5. What's the NEXT layer of failure?
**REQUIRED SECTION.** Speculate explicitly about what will surface AFTER the v0.35.0 fixes ship. Input to v0.36.0 planning. At least three candidates.

- **Candidate 1:** False-positive quarantine count after the first prod week. Severity: Medium. Why we suspect: the `applied_count>10 ∧ ratio<0.1` rule is calibrated on the v0.32.0 fixture (one obvious offender at `applied=940, succeeded=0`); real entries on a multi-project hive may legitimately have low success ratios because most tasks they touch don't need their advice. Falsifiable signal: > 5% of swarm entries flip to `quarantined=True` within the first week, OR an operator reports a useful entry was silently dropped from injection (the audit JSONL line shows ratio in (0.05, 0.10)).
- **Candidate 2:** Critic-evidence rejection rate per critic role. Severity: Medium. Why we suspect: the marker tuple is static and case-insensitive but doesn't catch every abstention shape critics produce in practice (e.g. an empty bullet list, or a 200-char body that's all infrastructure noise). Falsifiable signal: `critic_evidence_rejected` op count across the next 50 critic dispatches stays below 5% (under-aggressive) OR exceeds 40% (over-aggressive), measured per `family` so the dominant pattern is visible.
- **Candidate 3:** Post-decay confirmation distribution looks wrong. Severity: Low. Why we suspect: the new 7-day half-life is anchored on `last_applied_at` which only starts ticking after v0.35.0 upgrade — legacy entries from before v0.35 will appear "fresh" until they're next injected, which means the first injection round post-upgrade will rank legacy entries equivalently to brand-new ones regardless of historical timestamp. Falsifiable signal: ratio of legacy (pre-upgrade) entries ranked in the top 5 of every injection block exceeds 80% for the first week, normalizing toward the swarm composition by week 2.

## 6. Process / discipline notes
- The 7-day decay curve uses a fixed `DecayCurveConfig(half_life_days=7, floor=0.5)` baked into `_rank_with_ts`. v0.36 should consider exposing this through `KnowledgeConfig.decay_curves["confirmations_7d"]` as the operator-tunable knob (the lookup is already wired in `_decay_curve_for`).
- The quarantine evaluator runs inside the same lock as the `applied_count` increment but the ledger op is emitted outside (after the lock releases) to avoid ledger I/O blocking knowledge writes. This is intentional but means the audit JSONL line and the ledger op can land out-of-order under crash conditions; the audit JSONL is the canonical record.
